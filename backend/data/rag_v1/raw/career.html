What industry should I get into?

Now that you are looking for a career, how do you know which industry will be suitable for you and your skills?  The following websites will give you information about industries, salary benchmark, skills/ competencies and other relevant information in looking for a career path. 

  • Future of Jobs This link opens in a new window
    The annual report explores how jobs and skills will evolve.
  • O*NET OnLine This link opens in a new window
    Find, search, or browse across 900+ occupations based on your goals and needs. (for US market)
  • Market Insights & Salary Guide This link opens in a new window
    A data-driven analysis crafted from Ambition's recruitment data and the knowledge of their recruitment experts. The reports provide a better understanding of what is happening and what could be happening in the near future.

Skills, personalitiy and career path

What types of careers/fields could you get into based on your skills and personality?

  • Quizzes This link opens in a new window
    Various career quizzes here can help you explore career pathways and develop insights into career decision-making based on your interests and talents.
  • CLC - One-On-One Career Consultation Services (Undergraduate) This link opens in a new window
    The Centre's professional will help with CV review and advising, cover letter review, job application advising, job search skills, interview skills, career planning, internship guidance and opportunities, and further studies advising.

Looking for additional information?

If you want more information, please try the following:

1.  Look for books in CityUHK LibraryFind. 

Change the drop-down menu in CityUHK LibraryFind to 'subject' in the Advanced Search option and try the following subject terms:

  • Career development
  • Vocational guidance

2.  Conduct research on companies or industries you are interested in.  Consult our research guides:

  • Business Information for China
  • Business Information for Hong Kong and Shanghai
  • Company Information
  • Industry Information

3.  Visit the Career and Leadership Centre website. They have a section on Employability Enhancement Programmes.
